## Give the next story somewhere to start.

Fountainhead is a free screenwriting editor aimed at the next generation of filmmakers. Chris and Brian Zou are building a place for writers to turn an idea into a screenplay, with premium editing tools available for free.

It’s an example of the kind of work that belongs in the studio: a small team, a clear audience, and a software product built around something its creators care about.

## The idea, out in the world

A screenplay is the beginning of a much bigger project. Fountainhead focuses on that first step: giving a filmmaker a tool to start writing.
