Brown AI Studio is a **10-week summer fellowship** for Brown University students. Build and launch something of your own with **$5,000 per fellow**, AI tools, and mentorship from Brown alumni who build software.

<p><a class="button" href="{{ site.application_url | escape }}">Apply to the fellowship {% include arrow.html direction="diagonal" %}</a></p>

## What could you build?

A website or app. A game. A creative tool. Something that makes someone’s day a little easier. Your project needs a software component, and AI will help you bring it to life.

See what past fellows have made in [Products]({{ '/products/' | relative_url }}), or [meet the team]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) who can help you along the way.

## A good fit for the studio

- You’re a Brown University student with a dream that only you can bring to life.
- You’re building solo or with one teammate. Two-person teams live in the same city during the program.
- You can work full time on your product over the summer.
- You’ve built software on a team before, in a class like CS32 or outside the classroom.
- You’re ready to build, talk to users, and keep improving. A working prototype is strongly preferred.

<p><a class="text-link" href="{{ site.application_url | escape }}">Sound like you? Apply here {% include arrow.html direction="diagonal" %}</a></p>

## Come with a point of view

Before you apply, think about your audience, the problem they face or the story you want to tell, and what you’ve already tried. A prototype, a conversation with a potential user, or a lesson from an earlier project can be a useful place to start.
