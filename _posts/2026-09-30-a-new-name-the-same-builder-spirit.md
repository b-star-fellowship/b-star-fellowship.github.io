B* began with a simple invitation: **build something**.

That invitation is still at the center of what we do. Our program is becoming **Brown AI Studio**, and the students who join us will be **Brown AI Fellows**.

## Why a studio?

A studio is a place to do the work. To try an idea, see where it breaks, and make it better. To turn something you’ve been thinking about into something someone else can actually use.

That’s the spirit we want to bring to a summer of building. Small teams, focused work, and the chance to learn by putting a real project into the world.

## A new set of tools. Your own point of view.

AI tools can change how quickly you move from an idea to a working piece of software. But deciding what to build, understanding who needs it, and caring about the details are still the work.

Brown AI Studio brings those things together: a summer to focus, funding to support that focus, and weekly conversations to help you find the next useful step.

## The next thing starts with you

We’re excited by people who notice a problem and can’t help thinking about how to solve it. People who want to make something, show it to someone, and keep going.

If that sounds like you, [learn about becoming a Brown AI Fellow]({{ '/apply/' | relative_url }}). And if you’d like to see what a summer of building can lead to, [meet the projects]({{ '/products' | relative_url }}).
